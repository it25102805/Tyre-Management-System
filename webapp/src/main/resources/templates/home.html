How We Rebuild a Tyre

Three stages, tracked from start to finish.

Step 1

Inspect and prepare

The tyre is registered, checked for suitability and prepared for rebuilding.

Step 2

Rebuild

The new tread is applied and the production record is updated at each stage.

Step 3

Cure and final check

The tyre is cured and finished, then passes a final quality check.
